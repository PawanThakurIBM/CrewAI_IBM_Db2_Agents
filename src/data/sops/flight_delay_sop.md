# Flight Delay Standard Operating Procedure

**Document ID:** SOP-OPS-001  
**Version:** 3.2  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-15  
**Owner:** VP Flight Operations  

---

## 1. Purpose

This SOP establishes standardised procedures for managing flight delays across all AirConnect operations. It defines decision thresholds, communication timelines, coordination responsibilities, and escalation paths to ensure consistent, compliant, and passenger-centred handling of delay events.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to all AirConnect flights operating from or into any station. Covers delays from initial identification through final departure or cancellation decision.

---

## 3. Delay Classification

| Delay Band | Duration | Category |
|------------|----------|----------|
| Minor | < 30 minutes | Informational only |
| Short | 30 min – 2 hours | Standard handling |
| Medium | 2 – 5 hours | Enhanced passenger care |
| Extended | 5 – 12 hours | Full disruption protocol |
| Long | > 12 hours | Crisis management |

---

## 4. Initial Delay Notification — Timeline

**T+0 (Delay identified):**
- Operations Controller logs delay in OpsSystem with IATA delay code
- Duty Manager notified immediately

**T+15 minutes:**
- Gate agents briefed with latest ETD
- Passenger announcement issued at gate (first notification)
- Cabin crew informed via crew management system

**T+30 minutes:**
- SMS/email notification sent to all affected passengers
- Connecting flight protection triggered for passengers with connections < 60 minutes

**T+60 minutes:**
- Catering hold placed for aircraft
- Fuel plan revised by dispatch
- Station Manager formally notified if delay band reaches Medium

---

## 5. Decision Thresholds

### 5.1 Continue with Delay (Proceed)
- Cause is resolvable within 3 hours
- Weather forecast shows improvement
- Aircraft and crew availability confirmed
- No regulatory crew rest breach

### 5.2 Initiate Cancellation Review
- Delay exceeds or is forecast to exceed 5 hours with no clear resolution
- Aircraft AOG (Aircraft on Ground) with no substitute available within 4 hours
- Crew duty time breach unavoidable
- Airport closure or government-issued ground stop

### 5.3 Cancellation Authority
- Delays up to 3 hours: Duty Manager approval
- Delays 3–8 hours: Station Manager + Network Control Centre
- Delays > 8 hours or cancellation: VP Flight Operations sign-off required

---

## 6. Coordination Checklist

Upon delay reaching Medium classification (2+ hours), the following teams must be contacted:

- [ ] Ground Operations — gate hold, baggage re-scan
- [ ] Catering — revised meal service timing
- [ ] Fuel — updated fuel plan from dispatch
- [ ] Crew Scheduling — crew duty time check and swap if needed
- [ ] Passenger Services — voucher issuance authority confirmed
- [ ] Airport Authority — slot adjustment requested
- [ ] Engineering — confirm aircraft readiness ETA
- [ ] Contact Centre — brief agents on talking points

---

## 7. Passenger Communication Scripts

### 7.1 Initial Delay Announcement (< 2 hours)
> "Ladies and gentlemen, we regret to inform you that flight [AC-XXXX] to [destination] is experiencing a delay. Our revised estimated departure time is [ETD]. We apologise for the inconvenience and will provide updates every 30 minutes. Please remain available at gate [XX]."

### 7.2 Extended Delay Announcement (2–5 hours)
> "Ladies and gentlemen, due to [delay reason], flight [AC-XXXX] is delayed until approximately [ETD]. We are providing [meal vouchers/refreshments] and our team is here to assist with rebooking if required. Please approach the service desk or speak to our gate agents."

### 7.3 Cancellation Announcement
> "We regret to announce that flight [AC-XXXX] to [destination] has been cancelled due to [reason]. Our team will assist every passenger with rebooking on the next available service. Compensation entitlements will be processed automatically. We sincerely apologise for this disruption."

---

## 8. Escalation Matrix

| Situation | Escalate To | Response Time |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| Delay > 2 hours | Station Manager | 15 minutes |
| Delay > 5 hours | Network Control Centre | Immediate |
| Crew rest breach | Chief Pilot On-Call | Immediate |
| Media attention | Corporate Communications | 30 minutes |
| VIP passenger complaint | VP Passenger Experience | 1 hour |
| Regulatory inquiry | Legal & Compliance | 1 hour |

---

## 9. IATA Delay Code Reference

All delays must be coded using IATA AHM730 codes:

- **Codes 11–19:** Passenger / baggage related
- **Codes 61–69:** Flight crew / operational
- **Codes 71–79:** Weather
- **Codes 81–89:** Air traffic control
- **Codes 93:** Previous flight late arrival (reactionary)

Accurate coding is mandatory for regulatory reporting and performance analysis.

## 10. Documentation Requirements

- OpsSystem delay log with timestamps
- IATA delay code and sub-code
- Passenger communication log
- Voucher issuance record
- Crew duty time record
- Final delay report submitted within 24 hours of flight closure
