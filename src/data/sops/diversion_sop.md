# Flight Diversion Standard Operating Procedure

**Document ID:** SOP-OPS-002  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-15  
**Owner:** VP Flight Operations  

---

## 1. Purpose

This SOP governs procedures for mid-flight and pre-departure diversions. A diversion occurs when an aircraft must land at an airport other than the scheduled destination due to safety, weather, medical, or operational reasons.

---

## 2. Types of Diversion

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Pre-departure | Aircraft diverted before departure to alternate origin |
| En-route | Aircraft diverts to alternate airport mid-flight |
| Destination closure | Destination airport closes after departure |
| Medical | In-flight medical emergency requiring immediate landing |

---

## 3. Captain's Authority

The Pilot in Command (PIC) holds absolute authority over diversion decisions once airborne. Ground operations must not override or delay a captain's diversion declaration. Upon declaring a diversion:

1. Captain notifies ATC with diversion airport and souls on board
2. First Officer communicates with Operations Control Centre via ACARS or SATCOM
3. Cabin crew notified and briefed on alternate airport procedures
4. Fuel state assessed and MAYDAY/PAN-PAN declared if required

---

## 4. Ground Coordination at Alternate Airport

Upon receiving diversion notification, Network Control Centre (NCC) activates:

**Within 15 minutes of diversion declaration:**
- Contact alternate airport operations — confirm gate/apron availability
- Notify ground handler at alternate airport
- Order fuel for alternate airport (coordinate with dispatch for revised fuel plan)
- Arrange passenger buses if remote stand allocated
- Contact catering at alternate if extended ground time anticipated

**Within 30 minutes:**
- Arrange immigration and customs notifications (for international diversions)
- Coordinate replacement crew if current crew will exceed FDP at alternate
- Arrange hotel rooms if overnight at alternate is likely
- Brief Contact Centre on passenger communication

---

## 5. Passenger Handling at Alternate Airport

### 5.1 Immediate Arrival Actions
- Passengers must not deplane until customs/immigration clearance confirmed (international flights)
- Cabin crew to make announcements every 15 minutes with updates
- Meal/water service initiated if ground time exceeds 1 hour

### 5.2 Passenger Communication at Alternate
- All passengers notified of next step options:
  - Continue to destination on same aircraft (if delay < 4 hours)
  - Transfer to next AirConnect service from alternate (if available)
  - Ground transportation to destination (if distance ≤ 300 km)
  - Hotel accommodation + next day service (if overnight)

### 5.3 Passenger Rights at Alternate
- Passengers retain full rights under EU261/2004 or DGCA guidelines
- Meal entitlements apply from point of diversion
- Hotel entitlement applies if overnight stay required
- Full refund option available if passenger declines travel

---

## 6. Crew Duty Time Considerations

Crew Flight Duty Period (FDP) limits apply from the original sign-on time, not from diversion point. NCC crew scheduling must:

1. Calculate remaining FDP for all crew members immediately upon diversion
2. If FDP breach occurs before passengers can be transported to destination, initiate crew swap
3. Standby crew must be despatched to alternate airport immediately
4. Crew hotel accommodation arranged if swap crew cannot reach alternate within 4 hours

**FDP Extension Authority:** Chief Pilot may grant a 1-hour FDP extension under exceptional safety-justified circumstances only.

---

## 7. Recovery Procedures

### 7.1 Same Aircraft Recovery
- Engineering at alternate confirms aircraft airworthiness
- Fuel, catering, and cleaning completed
- Slot at destination re-requested from ATC
- Passengers re-boarded

### 7.2 Aircraft Cannot Continue
- Aircraft declared AOG at alternate
- Substitute aircraft sourced from nearest hub within 4 hours
- Ferry flight arranged if substitute unavailable at alternate
- Passengers transported by bus or partner airline if recovery time > 6 hours

---

## 8. Documentation

- Diversion report filed within 2 hours of landing at alternate
- Captain's report submitted within 24 hours
- All passenger communication logged in PSS
- Compensation records updated in passenger management system
