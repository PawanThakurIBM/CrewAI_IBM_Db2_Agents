# Flight Cancellation Standard Operating Procedure

**Document ID:** SOP-OPS-003  
**Version:** 2.4  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-15  
**Owner:** VP Flight Operations  

---

## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines the process for making, communicating, and managing flight cancellation decisions. A cancellation is the most disruptive outcome for passengers and carries significant regulatory and financial consequences. This SOP ensures cancellations are justified, documented, and managed with minimum passenger impact.

---

## 2. Cancellation Decision Authority

| Cancellation Type | Authority Level |
|-------------------|-----------------|
| Planned cancellation (schedule change) | Network Planning |
| Weather / operational < 24 hours | Station Manager + NCC |
| ATC / airport closure | Duty Manager (automatic) |
| Safety / technical | Captain / Chief Engineer |
| > 8 hours delay converted to cancel | VP Flight Operations |

Cancellations must never be declared without explicit authority from the appropriate level. The Duty Manager may declare a provisional cancellation pending VP sign-off in cases of obvious necessity (e.g., airport closed).

---

## 3. Trigger Conditions

A cancellation should be considered when:

1. Delay is forecast to exceed 5 hours and root cause is unresolved
2. Aircraft is AOG with no substitute available within 4 hours
3. Minimum crew complement cannot be assembled within legal FDP
4. Airport is officially closed (NOTAM or government order)
5. Weather at destination is below operating minima for all planned alternates
6. Security threat declared by airport authority

---

## 4. Pre-Cancellation Checklist

Before declaring cancellation, the following must be confirmed:

- [ ] All alternate routings explored (partner airline, next day, reroute)
- [ ] Substitute aircraft availability checked system-wide
- [ ] Crew swap possibility confirmed or ruled out
- [ ] Engineering recovery timeline confirmed as unachievable in required window
- [ ] VP authority obtained (for delays > 8 hours)
- [ ] Contact Centre briefed and ready for call volume spike
- [ ] Automated SMS/email notification system armed

---

## 5. Passenger Re-Accommodation Priority

Passengers must be reaccommodated in the following priority order:

1. **Medical cases (MEDA)** — immediate rebooking, ground transportation if required
2. **Unaccompanied Minors (UM)** — direct contact with parent/guardian before action
3. **Passengers with Disabilities (WCHR/WCHS/WCHC)** — specialist assistance arranged
4. **Premium cabin passengers** — Gold/Platinum tier and business class
5. **Tight connections** — passengers with onward connections < 3 hours
6. **Economy standard** — rebooking by ticket fare class (higher fare first)

---

## 6. Refund vs. Rebooking

**At passenger's choice:**

| Option | Conditions |
|--------|------------|
| Rebook — same carrier | Next available AirConnect flight to same destination |
| Rebook — partner airline | If AirConnect has no service within 8 hours |
| Full refund | If passenger declines rebooking |
| Partial refund | For unused portions only (connecting journey) |

Refunds must be processed within **7 calendar days** to original form of payment. Credit vouchers may not be offered as a substitute for cash/card refund unless passenger explicitly consents.

---

## 7. Station Manager Responsibilities

Upon cancellation declaration:

1. Activate cancellation protocol in DCS (Departure Control System)
2. Ensure gate agents are briefed and positioned
3. Set up rebooking desks with minimum 4 agents for flights > 150 passengers
4. Coordinate with airport authority for lounge access if available
5. Arrange ground transportation if hotel accommodation needed
6. Confirm meal vouchers issued before passengers leave the gate area
7. Submit cancellation report to NCC within 1 hour

---

## 8. Communication Sequence

| Stakeholder | Channel | Timing |
|-------------|---------|--------|
| Passengers — at airport | Gate announcement | Immediately |
| Passengers — SMS/Email | Automated via CRM | Within 5 minutes |
| Airport authority | Ops hotline | Within 15 minutes |
| Ground handler | Direct call | Within 15 minutes |
| Catering | Cancel order | Within 30 minutes |
| Fuel | Cancel uplift | Within 30 minutes |
| ATC | Slot cancellation | Immediately via dispatch |

---

## 9. Documentation Requirements

- Cancellation reason code (IATA AHM730)
- Passenger count by class
- Rebooking summary (# reaccommodated, # refunded, # pending)
- Voucher issuance total
- Timeline log (decision time, communication times, final closure)
- Post-incident report within 48 hours
