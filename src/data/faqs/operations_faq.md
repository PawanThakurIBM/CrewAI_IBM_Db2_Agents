# Operations FAQ — Ground Staff and Station Managers

**Document ID:** FAQ-OPS-001  
**Version:** 1.8  
**Owner:** VP Ground Operations / Network Control Centre  

---

## Q1: Who authorises a flight cancellation?

**A:** Cancellation authority depends on delay duration and context:

| Situation | Authorising Person |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Delay < 3 hours converted to cancel | Duty Manager |
| Delay 3–8 hours / operational issue | Station Manager + NCC Operations Controller |
| Delay > 8 hours | VP Flight Operations (sign-off required) |
| AOG / technical cause | Chief Engineer + NCC |
| Airport closure (NOTAM) | Duty Manager (automatic — document and notify VP) |

**Station Managers may NOT unilaterally cancel a flight** without NCC authorisation except in cases of clear safety-based airport closure. Always call NCC first.

## Q2: How do I handle a ground stop?

**A:** When you receive a ground stop notification:

1. Immediately enter the ground stop in OpsSystem with EDCT time(s)
2. Make a gate announcement within 10 minutes (use SOP-OPS-005 script)
3. Notify all gate agents covering affected flights
4. Contact catering to place the order on hold
5. Check crew FDP — if EDCT pushes total duty beyond limit, notify NCC for crew swap
6. Monitor ATFM system for EDCT changes every 15 minutes
7. When EDCT window is within 30 minutes: brief crew, confirm boarding completion

## Q3: What is the escalation path for a 3-hour delay?

**A:** When a delay reaches 3 hours:

1. **You (Gate Agent/Ops Agent):** Escalate to Station Manager and issue meal vouchers
2. **Station Manager:** Notify NCC formally (not just a quick call — log it)
3. **NCC Operations Controller:** Assess whether cancellation review is needed; contacts VP if delay trending > 5 hours
4. **Passenger Services:** Rebooking desk activated; agents briefed on alternatives
5. **Contact Centre:** Briefed on talking points for inbound calls

Simultaneously, you must:
- Confirm rebooking options are available before announcing to passengers
- Check that all special assistance passengers are identified and receiving care
- Update FIDS / display boards

## Q4: How do I coordinate with catering for an extended delay?

**A:** Catering coordination during delays:

| Delay Duration | Action |
|----------------|--------|
| < 60 minutes | No action — monitor |
| 60–90 minutes | Place catering on hold; advise new ETD |
| 90 minutes–3 hours | Confirm whether original order is still valid; may need cold replacement |
| > 3 hours | Place new catering order for revised departure time |
| Cancellation | Cancel catering immediately; failure to cancel costs the airline |

The catering manager needs minimum **90 minutes notice** to prepare a fresh hot meal service. For delays discovered late, cold alternatives are the only realistic option. Always confirm what catering can deliver before communicating to crew and passengers.

## Q5: A passenger is demanding compensation at the gate. What do I do?

**A:** At the gate, station agents do not issue cash compensation — that is handled by the Claims team. However, you must:

1. Acknowledge the passenger's frustration and listen
2. Confirm they have received their care entitlements (meal, communication)
3. Provide them with the **compensation claim form or URL**
4. Note their PNR and flag it as "compensation claim pending" in the remarks
5. Escalate to Station Manager if passenger is aggressive or media is involved

**Never promise a specific compensation amount verbally** — always direct to the formal claim process. Claims are verified against flight records before payment.

## Q6: What do I do if a passenger's bag cannot be transferred to the rebooking flight?

**A:** If a bag cannot be transferred (different carrier, no interline tag, time constraint):

1. Advise the passenger clearly and apologise
2. Issue a **Property Irregularity Report (PIR)** at the service desk
3. Bag will travel on the next available flight to the destination
4. Give the passenger their PIR reference number and the baggage tracing hotline
5. If the delay to their bag is > 24 hours: advise passenger they can claim essential items purchases (per baggage disruption policy, up to INR 3,000 / €50)

## Q7: What are my responsibilities if a passenger declares a medical emergency at the gate?

**A:** Gate agents are not medically trained — do not attempt diagnosis. Your responsibilities:

1. Call airport emergency services / medical team immediately (airport emergency number)
2. Keep the gate area clear — ask other passengers to step back
3. Do NOT allow the flight to board around an ongoing medical emergency
4. Notify Station Manager immediately
5. Log the event in OpsSystem with timestamps
6. Once medical team has the passenger, resume boarding with Station Manager approval
