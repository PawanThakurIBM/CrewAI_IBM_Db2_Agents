# Special Assistance Policy

**Document ID:** POL-PAX-005  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-15  
**Owner:** VP Passenger Experience / Ground Operations  

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the handling procedures for passengers requiring special assistance during flight disruptions. These passengers have elevated duty-of-care obligations and must be handled with priority regardless of the nature or cause of the disruption.

---

## 2. Special Assistance Categories (IATA SSR Codes)

| Code | Category | Description |
|------|----------|-------------|
| WCHR | Wheelchair — Ramp | Can walk on board, cannot manage stairs/long distances |
| WCHS | Wheelchair — Steps | Cannot manage stairs, can move in cabin |
| WCHC | Wheelchair — Cabin | Completely immobile, requires full assistance on/off aircraft |
| UMNR | Unaccompanied Minor | Child aged 5–12 (5–15 on some routes) travelling alone |
| MEDA | Medical case | Passenger with medical condition requiring airline clearance |
| BLND | Blind passenger | Requires orientation assistance |
| DEAF | Deaf/hard of hearing | Requires written communication |
| PETC | Emotional support / service animal | In-cabin assistance animal |

---

## 3. Wheelchair Handling at Alternate Airports During Diversion/Delay

When a disruption occurs and the affected airport changes (diversion or overnight hotel transfer):

### 3.1 WCHR/WCHS
- Airport wheelchair equipment must be pre-ordered from alternate airport ground handler before aircraft lands
- Lead time required: **45 minutes minimum** before arrival
- If alternate airport has no wheelchair equipment, airline must arrange accessible transport

### 3.2 WCHC Passengers
- WCHC passengers require an **aisle chair** for boarding/deboarding — this is specialised equipment
- NCC must confirm aisle chair availability at alternate airport BEFORE diversion decision if time permits
- Hotel accommodation: only accessible rooms accepted. Station manager must verify before booking
- Ground transport: adapted vehicle required — standard taxi is not acceptable

---

## 4. Unaccompanied Minor (UMNR) Protocols During Rebooking

During disruptions, UM handling is the highest duty-of-care obligation:

1. **Notification obligation:** Parent/guardian must be contacted BEFORE any rebooking action is taken
2. **Verbal + written consent** required from guardian for any routing change
3. UM must NEVER be left unaccompanied at any point — dedicated UM supervisor or escort required at all times
4. If rebooking requires overnight: **dedicated AirConnect staff escort** and child-appropriate accommodation (not adult hotel room) required
5. UM cannot be rebooked onto a partner airline unless that carrier has confirmed UM handling capability and parent has consented

**If UM guardian cannot be reached within 60 minutes:** escalate to Station Manager and AirConnect Child Protection Officer.

---

## 5. Medical Case (MEDA) Escalation

MEDA passengers must be assessed for travel fitness before rebooking:

1. If delay is short (< 3 hours) and condition is stable: standard rebooking applies
2. If passenger's condition may have changed due to delay/stress: request FIT-TO-FLY confirmation from airport medical facility
3. If passenger requires medical equipment (oxygen, stretcher): confirm availability on replacement aircraft BEFORE confirming rebooking
4. MEDA passengers travelling with medical escort: escort must also be reaccommodated on same flight

Escalation path for complex MEDA cases: Station Medical Officer → AirConnect Medical Desk (24-hr hotline).

---

## 6. Service Animal Handling

During disruption:
- Service animals remain with passenger at all times — relief areas at airport must be identified by ground staff
- If overnight hotel stay: confirm hotel accepts service animals BEFORE booking (non-negotiable)
- Relief area at hotel must be confirmed during hotel booking

---

## 7. Priority Boarding During Recovery

All special assistance passengers board BEFORE general boarding during recovery/replacement flights:
- WCHC → WCHS → WCHR → UMNR → MEDA → BLND/DEAF
- Gate agents must ensure jet bridge or ambulift is ready before boarding announcement

---

## 8. Documentation

- All special assistance disruption handling must be logged in PSS under PNR remarks
- WCHC hotel and transport arrangements logged with confirmation numbers
- UMNR: guardian communication log with timestamps
- MEDA: fit-to-fly assessment reference number
