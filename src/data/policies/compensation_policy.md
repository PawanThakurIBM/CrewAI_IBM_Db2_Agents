# Compensation Policy

**Document ID:** POL-PAX-002  
**Version:** 3.7  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-15  
**Owner:** VP Passenger Experience / Legal & Compliance  

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy provides the precise compensation rules, entitlement matrix, and calculation methodology that AirConnect agents and automated systems use to determine passenger compensation during flight disruptions.

---

## 2. Compensation Eligibility — Core Conditions

A passenger is entitled to financial compensation when ALL of the following apply:

1. The disruption (delay, cancellation, denied boarding) is **controllable** by AirConnect
2. The passenger held a **confirmed reservation** and checked in on time
3. The flight departs from an EU airport OR is operated by an EU carrier on an EU-arriving flight
4. The disruption is **not** an extraordinary circumstance (see policy POL-PAX-001)

---

## 3. Compensation Matrix

### 3.1 Delay at Destination

| Route Distance | Arrival Delay | Compensation |
|----------------|---------------|-------------|
| ≤ 1,500 km | ≥ 3 hours | **€250** |
| 1,500–3,500 km | ≥ 3 hours | **€400** |
| > 3,500 km | 3–4 hours | **€300** (50% reduction applies) |
| > 3,500 km | ≥ 4 hours | **€600** |

**India domestic (DGCA):**

| Delay Band | Compensation |
|------------|-------------|
| 2–3 hours | INR 5,000 or full refund (passenger's choice) |
| 3–6 hours | INR 7,500 |
| > 6 hours | INR 10,000 or full refund |

### 3.2 Cancellation Compensation
Same matrix as Section 3.1, triggered by arrival delay caused by cancellation (see POL-PAX-001 Section 3).

### 3.3 Denied Boarding
Same matrix as Section 3.1, triggered immediately upon involuntary denied boarding regardless of later flight offered.

---

## 4. Care Entitlements (Always Apply, Regardless of Cause)

### 4.1 Meals and Refreshments

| Delay Duration | Entitlement |
|----------------|------------|
| < 2 hours | None |
| 2–3 hours | Snack / light refreshment (value: up to €5 / INR 400) |
| 3–5 hours | Full meal voucher (value: up to €12 / INR 800) |
| > 5 hours | Full meal + subsequent meals if overnight (€15–20 / INR 1,000–1,500 per meal) |

### 4.2 Hotel Accommodation
Provided when:
- Delay requires an overnight stay AND
- Passenger is away from their airport of residence

Hotel standard: minimum 3-star or equivalent; up to €100/night international, INR 3,500/night domestic.

### 4.3 Transportation
Return transfers between hotel and airport are the airline's responsibility at all times.

### 4.4 Communication
Two free telephone calls, email, fax, or telex. If at airport, Wi-Fi voucher acceptable equivalent.

---

## 5. Extraordinary Circumstances — Exclusions

When extraordinary circumstances are confirmed:
- **Care entitlements (meals, hotel, transport, communication) STILL APPLY**
- **Financial compensation (€250/400/600) is WAIVED**

Agents must not deny meal vouchers based on extraordinary circumstances — care is always owed.

---

## 6. Voucher Issuance Process

1. Passenger Services agent confirms delay duration in DCS
2. Agent selects appropriate voucher denomination in COMP system
3. Voucher issued digitally (SMS/email) or as paper at counter
4. All issuances logged against PNR in PSS for reconciliation

**Voucher validity:** 12 months from date of issue. Non-transferable. Cannot be redeemed for cash.

---

## 7. Cash Compensation Payment Process

1. Passenger submits claim at airport desk or via website
2. Claims team verifies eligibility against flight records (OTP, IATA delay code, cause)
3. Approved claims paid within 14 days via:
   - Original payment method (bank transfer, credit card refund)
   - No travel vouchers as substitute unless passenger explicitly agrees
4. Denied claims: written explanation issued within 14 days

---

## 8. Total Disruption Cost Estimation

For budgeting purposes, agents use the following per-flight estimates:

| Passenger Count | 1-hour delay cost | 3-hour delay cost (full care) | Cancellation cost |
|-----------------|-------------------|-------------------------------|-------------------|
| 100–150 pax | €500–750 | €2,500–5,000 | €35,000–50,000 |
| 150–250 pax | €750–1,250 | €5,000–10,000 | €55,000–90,000 |
| 250–350 pax | €1,250–1,800 | €10,000–15,000 | €90,000–130,000 |

These are estimates for operations planning. Legal team must validate final amounts.
