# Refund Policy

**Document ID:** POL-PAX-004  
**Version:** 2.3  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-15  
**Owner:** VP Revenue / VP Passenger Experience  

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the circumstances under which passengers are entitled to a refund, the calculation methodology, and the processing timelines AirConnect must meet.

---

## 2. Full Refund Triggers

A passenger is entitled to a **full refund** of the ticket price (base fare + taxes + fees) when:

1. AirConnect cancels the flight
2. Delay exceeds 5 hours and passenger elects not to travel
3. AirConnect fails to transport passenger to destination within reasonable time due to mishandling
4. Involuntary downgrade occurs and passenger declines travel in lower cabin
5. AirConnect changes schedule by > 3 hours and passenger is not satisfied with offered alternative
6. Flight is diverted to a city other than the ticketed destination and passenger elects not to continue

---

## 3. Partial Refund Rules

### 3.1 Voluntary Refunds (Passenger-Initiated)
Subject to fare rules applicable at time of purchase:
- Fully flexible tickets: 100% refund, no penalty
- Semi-flexible: refund minus change/cancel fee stated in fare conditions
- Non-refundable: taxes and airport charges refunded; base fare forfeited

### 3.2 Disruption Partial Refund
For passengers who have completed part of a journey before disruption (e.g., leg 1 flown, leg 2 disrupted):
- Refund amount = unused portion's proportional value of total ticket price
- Taxes and surcharges for unused segments fully refunded
- Calculation method: prorate by mileage (IATA prorating rules)

---

## 4. Ancillary Refund Rules

When a refund is issued for a disrupted flight:

| Ancillary Item | Refund Treatment |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Seat selection fee | Fully refunded |
| Prepaid excess baggage | Fully refunded if bags not transported |
| Lounge access pass (single use) | Fully refunded if not used |
| Meal pre-order | Fully refunded |
| Travel insurance | NOT refunded (separate product — insurer terms apply) |
| Frequent flyer miles (paid with miles) | Miles reinstated within 5 business days |

---

## 5. Refund Timeline Commitments

| Refund Type | Processing Target |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Credit/debit card refund | 7 calendar days from claim approval |
| Bank transfer | 10 business days |
| Cash (at airport) | Same day for amounts ≤ INR 5,000 / €100 |
| Original form of payment (general) | Within 7 calendar days (EU261/DGCA requirement) |

**AirConnect must not delay refunds beyond 7 calendar days where EU261/2004 or DGCA rules apply.** Failure to meet this timeline exposes AirConnect to regulatory fines.

---

## 6. Original Form of Payment Rule

Refunds must be made to the **original form of payment** used for purchase:
- Credit card purchase → credit to same card
- Cash purchase → cash or bank transfer
- Travel agent purchase → refund via original GDS transaction

**Travel vouchers may not be substituted** for cash/card refunds unless the passenger explicitly provides written consent. Verbal consent is not sufficient.

---

## 7. Involuntary Refund — Process

1. Passenger presents at service desk or contacts customer care
2. Agent verifies PNR, disruption code, and passenger's right to refund
3. Agent initiates refund in PSS / ticketing system
4. Confirmation sent to passenger immediately by email
5. Refund processed within timeline per Section 5

Supervisory override required for refunds exceeding INR 50,000 / €600 per passenger.

---

## 8. Refund Denial

Refunds may be denied when:
- Flight departed and passenger was a no-show (not disruption-caused)
- Non-refundable ticket and no disruption event occurred
- Claim submitted beyond limitation period (2 years India, 6 years EU)
- Fraudulent claim suspected (refer to Revenue Protection team)

Denied claims must be communicated in writing within 14 days with specific reason.
