# Rebooking Policy

**Document ID:** POL-PAX-003  
**Version:** 2.9  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-15  
**Owner:** VP Passenger Experience  

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the rules, priority order, and procedures for rebooking passengers during flight disruptions caused by delays, cancellations, or diversions.

---

## 2. Priority Order for Rebooking

Passengers must be reaccommodated in the following strict priority order:

| Priority | Category | Reason |
|----------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Medical cases (MEDA) | Safety-critical, requires immediate action |
| 2 | Unaccompanied Minors (UM) | Legal and duty of care obligation |
| 3 | Passengers with Disabilities (WCHR, WCHS, WCHC) | Regulatory requirement, special logistics |
| 4 | Premium cabin (Business/First) + Elite tier (Gold/Platinum FFP) | Contract obligation |
| 5 | Passengers with onward connections < 2 hours | Cascade disruption prevention |
| 6 | Economy — higher fare classes first (Y, B, H before Q, V, G) | Revenue management |
| 7 | Economy — lower fare classes | Standard queue |

Agents must not skip priority levels. If a higher-priority passenger cannot be accommodated on the same flight as a lower-priority passenger, the higher-priority passenger is moved first.

---

## 3. Rebooking Options

### 3.1 Same Carrier Rebooking
- Priority: next available AirConnect flight to same destination
- Same cabin class as original booking (if available)
- If same cabin unavailable: upgrade to available higher cabin at no charge
- Downgrade: only with passenger consent and partial refund of fare difference

### 3.2 Partner Airline Rebooking
Permitted when:
- No AirConnect service available within 8 hours of disrupted flight's scheduled departure
- Partner airline has interline or code-share agreement with AirConnect
- Relevant interline e-ticket agreement is active

**Partner airlines for reaccommodation (primary):**
- Star Alliance member carriers
- AirConnect bilateral partners (list maintained by Revenue Management)

### 3.3 Voluntary Rebooking
If a passenger chooses to travel on a later date voluntarily:
- No change fees apply during disruption period
- Valid for rebooking within 72 hours of disruption event
- Fare difference: waived if disruption is AirConnect-caused; passenger pays difference if voluntary

### 3.4 Refund in Lieu of Rebooking
Passenger may decline any rebooking and request full refund:
- Must be processed within 7 calendar days
- Refund to original form of payment
- If passenger accepts hotel and meals while deciding, refund still applies

---

## 4. No-Show Protection During Disruption

Passengers who miss onward connections or next-segment flights due to an AirConnect disruption are automatically protected:
- No-show penalty waived
- PNR remains active for 72 hours
- Rebooking agents have authority to reinstate without supervisor approval

---

## 5. Endorsement Rules

To rebook on another carrier, an endorsement must be added to the ticket:
- AirConnect tickets are by default **non-endorsable**
- During IATA-declared irregular operations, Operations Control Centre issues a global endorsement authorisation covering all affected flights
- Station agents may not endorse without NCC authorisation code
- Endorsement format: `ENDORSEMENT AUTH [NCC-CODE] [DATE]`

---

## 6. Seat Assignment During Rebooking

- Passengers retain their original seat class entitlement
- Window/aisle preferences honoured where available
- Families must not be separated if adjacent seats exist on replacement flight
- WCHR/WCHC passengers: bulkhead or accessible row seats required on replacement flight

---

## 7. Communication to Rebooking Passengers

When issuing new boarding pass:
1. Clearly state new flight number, departure time, gate, and aircraft type
2. Confirm checked baggage transfer (advise if bags cannot follow — new claim tag required)
3. Advise of lounge access rights if applicable
4. Provide contact details for further rebooking changes

---

## 8. Rebooking Completion Targets

| Disruption Size | Target Completion Time |
|-----------------|------------------------|
| < 50 passengers | 45 minutes from declaration |
| 50–150 passengers | 90 minutes |
| 150–300 passengers | 3 hours |
| > 300 passengers | 4 hours + NCC escalation |
