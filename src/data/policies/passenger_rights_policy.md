# Passenger Rights Policy

**Document ID:** POL-PAX-001  
**Version:** 5.1  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-15  
**Owner:** VP Passenger Experience  

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines AirConnect's commitments to passengers during flight disruptions, aligned with EU Regulation 261/2004, DGCA Passenger Charter (India), and AirConnect's own service standards. Where AirConnect's standards exceed regulatory minimums, AirConnect's standards apply.

---

## 2. Rights During Flight Delay

### 2.1 Delays Under 2 Hours
- Passengers are informed of the delay and revised ETD
- No compensation or vouchers required

### 2.2 Delays of 2–3 Hours
- **Meal/refreshment voucher** issued: INR 250 (domestic) / USD 5 equivalent (international)
- Two telephone calls, emails, or faxes at airline expense (or equivalent data access)
- Passengers informed of right to claim if delay is controllable

### 2.3 Delays of 3–5 Hours
- **Meal voucher** (upgraded): INR 500 (domestic) / USD 12 equivalent (international)
- Two telephone calls or equivalent
- Rebooking assistance offered for passengers with onward connections

### 2.4 Delays Over 5 Hours
- All rights from 2.3 above, plus:
- Passenger may elect **full refund** instead of continued travel
- If passenger chooses to continue, **hotel accommodation** provided for overnight delays
- Ground transportation to/from hotel arranged by airline

### 2.5 Overnight Delay (any cause)
- Hotel accommodation: up to INR 3,500 (domestic) / USD 100 (international) per night
- Meals: breakfast, lunch, and dinner per applicable meal window
- Return transfers between hotel and airport

---

## 3. Rights During Flight Cancellation

### 3.1 Immediate Entitlements
- Full information about cancellation and cause
- Choice of: (a) rebooking on next available service, or (b) full refund

### 3.2 Compensation (Controllable Cancellations Only)
Cancellations not caused by extraordinary circumstances entitle passengers to:

| Route Distance | Compensation |
|----------------|-------------|
| Up to 1,500 km | €250 / INR 7,500 |
| 1,500–3,500 km | €400 / INR 12,000 |
| Over 3,500 km | €600 / INR 18,000 |

Compensation is reduced by 50% if the airline offers rerouting and the passenger arrives within 2 hours (short routes) or 4 hours (long routes) of scheduled arrival.

### 3.3 Notice Periods Affecting Compensation
- Cancellation notified > 14 days before departure: **no compensation**
- Cancellation notified 7–14 days before + alternate offered within 2/4 hours: **no compensation**
- Cancellation notified < 7 days / at airport: **full compensation applies**

---

## 4. Rights During Denied Boarding

If AirConnect involuntarily denies boarding due to overbooking:

1. Volunteers sought first — compensation offered is a commercial decision
2. Involuntary denied boarding triggers same compensation as cancellation (table above)
3. Passenger immediately offered rebooking or full refund
4. Written notice of rights provided at gate

---

## 5. Distinction: Extraordinary Circumstances

The following are classified as **extraordinary circumstances** — compensation is not payable but care entitlements (meals, hotel) still apply:

- Severe weather (Met Office / METAR declared)
- ATC restrictions, airspace closures
- Airport infrastructure failure (not airline-caused)
- Security threats, government-ordered restrictions
- Medical emergency declared on aircraft
- Bird strike / wildlife collision (first occurrence)

The following are **NOT** extraordinary circumstances:
- Technical faults within airline's control
- Crew shortage due to scheduling error
- IT system failures
- Fuel shortages

---

## 6. How to Claim

Passengers may submit claims:
- At airport service desk immediately
- Via AirConnect website within 6 years (EU) / 2 years (India) of travel date
- Through designated airline handling agent

AirConnect targets processing all claims within **14 calendar days**.
