# Crew Operations Manual — Disruption Procedures

**Document ID:** MAN-CREW-001  
**Version:** 6.2  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-15  
**Owner:** Chief Pilot / VP Cabin Operations  

---

## 1. Purpose

This manual section defines crew duties, FDP management, communication procedures, and service obligations during flight disruptions. All crew — flight deck and cabin — must be familiar with these procedures.

---

## 2. Flight Duty Period (FDP) Limits

FDP begins at **sign-on time** (typically 60–90 minutes before scheduled departure) and ends at block-off of the final sector.

### 2.1 Maximum FDP by Acclimatisation State

| Crew Acclimatisation | Sectors | Max FDP |
|---------------------|---------|---------|
| Fully acclimatised | 1–2 | 13 hours |
| Fully acclimatised | 3–4 | 12 hours |
| Not acclimatised | 1–2 | 11.5 hours |
| Night duty (22:00–07:00 covering 02:00) | 1–2 | 11 hours |

### 2.2 Rest Requirements Triggering Crew Swap

A crew swap is **mandatory** when:

1. Remaining FDP (from current time to estimated block-in) < required minimum rest period
2. Crew have already worked maximum daily hours
3. Off-duty rest before sign-on was less than minimum required (10 hours minimum, 12 hours preferred for long-haul)
4. Crew member declares fatigue to Purser/Captain (non-punitive reporting — always honoured)

**Crew swap lead time: 2–3 hours.** NCC must be notified at T-3 hours of projected breach, not at T-0.

---

## 3. Cabin Crew Briefing Procedures During Delay

When ground delay exceeds 30 minutes, the Purser must:

1. Brief all cabin crew on:
   - Known delay reason (to the extent disclosable)
   - Estimated delay duration
   - Passenger service obligations per delay band
   - Any special passengers on board (MEDA, UM, WCHC)

2. Confirm communication cadence: passenger announcements every 20–30 minutes

3. Brief crew on voucher issuance threshold (advise gate agent when triggered)

---

## 4. IFE and Catering During Extended Ground Delay

### 4.1 Inflight Entertainment (IFE)
- IFE may be activated on ground during delays > 60 minutes at Captain's discretion
- APU fuel burn is approximately 200 kg/hour — dispatch must be consulted for delays > 2 hours
- IFE activation requires ground power connection to avoid excessive fuel burn

### 4.2 Catering During Delay
- Original catering order held for up to 90 minutes before expiry concern
- If delay extends beyond 90 minutes, new catering order must be placed
- Hot food may not be re-heated on ground — cold snack service only for > 90 min ground delays
- Water service every 30 minutes if passengers remain on board during delay > 1 hour

### 4.3 Passenger Deplaning During Long Ground Delay
If delay exceeds 3 hours on ground with no imminent departure, passengers should be offered option to deplane:
- Gate must be available
- Passengers informed they may be reboarded at short notice
- Security re-screening required for passengers who deplane in international terminal

---

## 5. Crew Communication Hierarchy During Disruption

| Decision Level | Who Decides |
|----------------|-------------|
| Passenger service on-board (water, snacks) | Purser |
| Communication with passengers | Purser (coordinates with Captain) |
| Deplane decision | Captain (Gate coordination required) |
| Crew rest / fatigue call | Captain (report to NCC) |
| Diversion decision | Captain (absolute authority) |
| Cancellation on ground | Station Manager + NCC (not crew) |

All crew communications to passengers must be coordinated: Purser handles cabin; Captain makes PA from flight deck for critical announcements.

---

## 6. Crew Hotel Accommodation

During disruption requiring overnight stay:
- Minimum room standard: 3-star hotel, single occupancy per crew member
- Travel time to hotel must not exceed 30 minutes (otherwise, in-airport accommodation)
- Wake-up calls arranged by hotel — crew must also set personal alarms
- Minimum rest at hotel before recall: 8 hours from room key to recall notification

---

## 7. Crew Return-to-Duty After Disruption

Following a disruption-related overnight:
- Crew scheduling issues revised report time
- Original sign-on time is reset — FDP clock restarts from new sign-on
- Any crew member who has exceeded cumulative weekly/monthly limits must be rested out — no exceptions
