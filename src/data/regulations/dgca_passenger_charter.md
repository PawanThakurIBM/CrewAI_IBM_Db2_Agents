# DGCA Passenger Charter — India

**Document ID:** REG-DGCA-001  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Reference:** DGCA CAR Section 3, Series M, Part IV  
**Owner:** Legal & Compliance  

---

## 1. Overview

The Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) of India mandates minimum passenger rights standards for all flights operating within India and for international flights departing from Indian airports on Indian carriers. These rules are published under the Civil Aviation Requirements (CAR) and are enforceable by DGCA.

---

## 2. Scope

Applies to:
- All scheduled domestic flights within India
- International flights departing India operated by Indian carriers
- International flights arriving India where code-share involves an Indian carrier

---

## 3. Compensation for Domestic Flight Delays

**Denied Boarding (Involuntary):**

| Alternative Flight Offered | Compensation |
|---------------------------|-------------|
| Alternate flight departing within 1 hour | INR 200 |
| Alternate flight departing within 1–24 hours | 200% of one-way basic fare (max INR 10,000) |
| No alternate flight offered | 400% of one-way basic fare (max INR 20,000) |

**Delay at Origin:**

| Delay Duration | Airline Obligation |
|----------------|-------------------|
| < 2 hours | No obligation beyond information |
| 2–3 hours | Meals and refreshments |
| 3–6 hours | Meals + INR 5,000 or full refund (passenger choice) |
| > 6 hours | Hotel + meals + INR 10,000 or full refund |

---

## 4. Tarmac Delay Rules (India)

If an aircraft is held on tarmac (on ground, doors closed):

- **30 minutes:** Passengers must be informed of cause and expected departure time
- **60 minutes:** Potable water and working lavatories must be available
- **90 minutes:** Food and refreshments must be available
- **2 hours:** Passengers must be given the option to deplane

**Failure to deplane passengers within 2 hours of ATC clearance delay = regulatory violation.**

AirConnect must report all tarmac delays > 2 hours to DGCA within 24 hours.

---

## 5. Cancellation Rights (India)

### 5.1 Cancellation by Airline
- Full refund within 30 days of cancellation event
- If cancellation within 2 weeks: refund within 7 days
- Alternative flight must be offered on next available service
- Compensation per denied boarding table if no alternate within 6 hours

### 5.2 Passenger-Initiated Cancellation
- Flexible fares: full refund within 30 days
- Non-refundable fares: taxes and airport charges only

---

## 6. Refund Timelines Under DGCA

| Payment Method | Maximum Refund Processing Time |
|----------------|-------------------------------|
| Credit/debit card | 7 calendar days |
| Net banking | 10 business days |
| Cash | Immediately (up to INR 5,000 at airport) |
| Travel agent | 30 days via agent |

DGCA requires airlines to publish actual processing times on their website and take corrective action if averages exceed these limits.

---

## 7. Applicability to International Departures from India

For international flights departing India:
- DGCA rules apply to denied boarding and tarmac delays
- EU261/2004 applies if flight arrives in EU (and carrier is EU-based)
- For Indian carriers on international routes departing India: DGCA + ICAO Annex 9 passenger facilitation guidelines apply
- In case of conflict, the more passenger-favourable rule applies

---

## 8. Passenger Complaint Process (India)

Passengers may complain to:
1. Airline's internal grievance officer (mandatory to have one per DGCA rules)
2. DGCA Facilitation Desk: **1800-11-0066** (toll free)
3. Ministry of Civil Aviation: **pgportal.gov.in**
4. Consumer Forum under Consumer Protection Act 2019
5. Civil Aviation Ombudsman (where appointed)

Airlines must respond to DGCA-referred complaints within **15 working days**.
