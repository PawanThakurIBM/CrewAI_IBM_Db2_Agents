# EU Regulation 261/2004 — Summary Reference

**Document ID:** REG-EU-261  
**Version:** 1.0 (based on EC 261/2004 as amended)  
**Effective Date:** 2004-02-17 (applied from 2005-02-17)  
**Owner:** Legal & Compliance  

---

## 1. Overview

EU Regulation 261/2004 establishes common rules on compensation and assistance to passengers in the event of denied boarding, cancellation, or long delay. It applies to:
- All flights **departing from** an EU/EEA airport
- Flights **arriving at** an EU/EEA airport operated by an **EU carrier**
- Passengers with a confirmed reservation who present for check-in on time

---

## 2. Article 5 — Cancellation

A passenger whose flight is cancelled is entitled to:

1. **Information** about the cancellation immediately
2. **Choice between:**
   - Refund within 7 days
   - Re-routing at earliest opportunity
   - Re-routing at a later date at passenger's convenience
3. **Right to care** (Article 9): meals, accommodation, communication
4. **Compensation** (Article 7) — unless:
   - Notified > 14 days before departure
   - Notified 7–14 days + re-routing within ±2/4 hours of original schedule
   - Notified < 7 days + re-routing within ±1/2 hours of original schedule
   - Caused by extraordinary circumstances

---

## 3. Article 6 — Delay

For delays at departure, passengers must be offered care (Article 9) when:

| Flight Distance | Delay Threshold for Care |
|-----------------|--------------------------|
| ≤ 1,500 km | ≥ 2 hours |
| 1,500–3,500 km | ≥ 3 hours |
| > 3,500 km | ≥ 4 hours |

**Important:** Article 6 does not trigger cash compensation for delay at departure. Cash compensation is triggered by Sturgeon ruling (CJEU C-402/07) when arrival delay is ≥ 3 hours.

---

## 4. Article 7 — Right to Compensation

Cash compensation amounts:

| Route Distance | Compensation Amount |
|----------------|---------------------|
| Up to 1,500 km | **€250** |
| 1,501–3,500 km | **€400** |
| Intra-EU > 1,500 km | **€400** |
| All other routes > 3,500 km | **€600** |

**50% reduction:** applicable when the airline offers re-routing and the passenger arrives within:
- 2 hours of scheduled arrival (for routes ≤ 1,500 km)
- 3 hours of scheduled arrival (for routes 1,500–3,500 km)
- 4 hours of scheduled arrival (for routes > 3,500 km)

---

## 5. Article 8 — Right to Reimbursement or Re-routing

When a flight is cancelled or a passenger is denied boarding:

**Option A — Full refund:**
- Full cost of ticket within 7 days
- Return flight to first point of departure if journey is pointless (e.g., outbound leg cancelled mid-trip)

**Option B — Re-routing at earliest opportunity:**
- Under comparable transport conditions
- No additional cost to passenger

**Option C — Re-routing at later date:**
- At passenger's convenience
- Subject to seat availability

---

## 6. Article 9 — Right to Care

Triggered by delay thresholds (see Article 6) AND cancellation AND denied boarding:

| Entitlement | Condition |
|-------------|-----------|
| Meals and refreshments | In reasonable relation to waiting time |
| Hotel accommodation | When stay of ≥ 1 night necessary |
| Transport to/from hotel | Always when hotel is provided |
| Two phone calls / emails / faxes | Always |

**Care must be provided even during extraordinary circumstances.** Airlines cannot refuse meals/hotel by citing weather or ATC.

---

## 7. Extraordinary Circumstances

The EU Court of Justice has clarified extraordinary circumstances include:
- Air traffic management decisions causing long delays
- Political instability, extreme weather, security risks
- Strikes external to the airline (ATC strike, airport staff strike)
- Hidden manufacturing defects (first occurrence, not within reasonable maintenance)

**Not extraordinary:**
- Technical problems due to failure of routine maintenance
- Crew shortage from scheduling
- Airline IT system failure

---

## 8. Enforcement

Passengers may enforce rights via:
- National Enforcement Body (NEB) in each EU member state
- Civil court claim (small claims court in most EU jurisdictions)
- Alternative dispute resolution (ADR) / ombudsman
- Limitation period: varies by jurisdiction (typically 2–6 years)
